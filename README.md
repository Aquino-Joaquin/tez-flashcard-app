# Flashcard-App

> A flashcard and study app that lets you create cards from anything you read or watch, and review them anywhere.

Graduation thesis project (Tez I) – Computer Engineering, Pamukkale University.

## About

Learners who use flashcards today usually juggle several tools: one to read PDFs or watch videos, another to write cards, and an AI tool to generate content. Text in scanned books, images and videos can't be copied easily, formatted content breaks on mobile, card layouts are rigid, and many apps are overloaded with features.

**Flashcard-App** brings all of this into one simple, relaxing app that works on both web and mobile.

## Key Features

- **Click to add**: double-click or tap any text (including scanned PDFs and images) to turn it into a flashcard.
- **Question generation**: generate questions and cards from university materials.
- **Rich cards**: Markdown, code blocks, tables and images that stay readable on mobile.
- **Flexible layouts**: choose what goes on the front and back, highlight text, use collapsible sections and color-code subjects.
- **Smart scheduling**: spaced repetition plus Google Calendar integration with exam countdowns.
- **Media capture**: save words and phrases from videos and subtitles.
- **Study habit helper**: open your flashcards instead of distracting apps.
- **Garden progress**: a garden that grows as you study.

## Who It's For

- Language learners who study with books, PDFs and series
- University students managing notes and technical content in several languages
- Professionals preparing for high-volume exams (e.g. medical licensing exams)
